02

Pick your starting recipe

Coverage and penalties stay adjustable for every category.

  • Starter recipes catch 6 messages in 8 seconds and block Discord invites. Other website links stay allowed.
  • Balanced adds racism, misogyny, and anti-trans hate filters at level 2, with message removal and a warning.
  • Starter recipes use a 10-minute timeout after 3 warnings within 24 hours. Both recipes preserve your custom term lists and role exemptions.

These are English word and phrase rules for moderation. Context, quotation, or reclaimed language can produce false positives. Review your logs and adjust coverage from 1–5, actions, extra terms, and exceptions in the panel.

Make room for people, not hate

Moderate identity-based abuse with separate controls for each category.

Choose coverage from 1–5 and an action for each filter. Levels add rules; choose the usual category action separately. Unicode-disguised matches use the timeout policy below. These English starter rules match words and phrases, not intent. Quoted or reclaimed language can match too—review your logs and tune carefully.

Unicode-disguised hate → immediate timeout. This applies when Unicode disguises occur inside a matched abuse rule, using your configured timeout duration. Ordinary emoji, accented names, and other languages do not trigger a rule on their own. Observation mode, exemptions, role hierarchy, and longer existing timeouts still apply.

06

A reaction, a role, a little belonging

Let members opt into community roles by reacting to a message.

Choose an existing message, emoji, and non-staff role. You need Manage Server and Manage Roles; Espresso needs Manage Roles and a role above the ones it assigns. Each role can have one mapping.

Start with a role template

Choose a channel and a starter menu, then review the options. Espresso posts an embed, adds reactions, and connects them to roles. All identity labels are optional and chosen by members.

Uncheck options you do not need. Choose an existing non-staff role explicitly, or create a basic role with no extra permissions. Country / region is a starter list; edit labels or add places. Up to 20 reactions per message and 25 mappings per server. Save any other settings first.

Open the posted message in Discord ↗

Posting turns reaction roles on for this server, including existing mappings. Members can select multiple roles; this is not a single-choice menu. Screening access roles cannot be granted here. Removing a mapping later keeps the embed and existing member roles.

Use an existing message instead

To copy a message ID, turn on Discord's Developer Mode, then right-click the message. Removing a reaction can remove a role granted by Espresso. Roles a member already had are left alone. Disabling or deleting a mapping does not remove existing roles.

07

A thoughtful welcome

Screen new accounts, collect rules agreement, and review who gets access.

Set channel access in Discord first: let @everyone see your entry channel, hide protected categories from @everyone, and allow the Verified role to view them. Espresso withholds the role; it does not change channel permissions or remove existing roles. Other bots must not automatically grant access roles.

Save before posting the verification button below. Members can also use /verify. Requests wait for rules agreement and any required staff review. Declining keeps access withheld without kicking or banning. Community reaction roles are available after verification.
