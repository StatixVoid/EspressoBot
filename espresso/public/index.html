Clear boundaries, carefully chosen

Moderation with
more than an on/off switch.

Adjust racism, misogyny, and anti-trans hate filters separately. Pick a level from 1–5, choose the action, and fine-tune each category with your own blocked terms and exact-match exceptions.

  1. 1 · CoreExplicit slur rules.
  2. 2 · ExpandedMore slur vocabulary.
  3. 3 · PhrasesDiscriminatory and exclusionary phrases.
  4. 4 · EvasionsSpacing, repetition, leetspeak, and selected Unicode disguises.
  5. 5 · BroaderMore identity-directed insults and stereotypes, including combined evasions.

Higher levels add coverage, not harsher penalties. These are English starter rules for moderation, not a judgment of a person's beliefs or intent. Context, quotes, and reclaimed language need human review.

Before your first cup

A few things
you might wonder.

Can I look around before inviting Espresso?

Yes. The public panel preview uses example servers and activity. You can explore the controls without changing a real Discord server.

Do I have to use every feature?

No. Choose what each server needs. Moderation, screening, roles, and community features have their own controls. The guided setup helps you start small.

Who can configure the server?

Owners and members with Manage Server or Administrator can configure it. Moderators with Moderate Members have a limited Staff desk. Role changes also require the relevant Manage Roles permission and role placement.

Does member screening hide channels automatically?

Prepare channel permissions in Discord first: keep the entry channel visible and grant protected access to your Verified role. Espresso withholds that role until your configured checks pass; it does not rewrite your channel permissions or remove existing roles.
