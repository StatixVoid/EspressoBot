Clear boundaries, carefully chosen

Moderation with
more than an on/off switch.

Adjust racism, misogyny, and anti-trans hate filters separately. Pick a level from 1–5, choose the action, and fine-tune each category with your own blocked terms and exact-match exceptions.

  1. 1 · CoreExplicit slur rules.
  2. 2 · ExpandedMore slur vocabulary.
  3. 3 · PhrasesDiscriminatory and exclusionary phrases.
  4. 4 · EvasionsCommon spacing, punctuation, and leetspeak variants.
  5. 5 · BroaderMore identity-directed insults and stereotypes.

Higher levels add coverage, not harsher penalties. These are English starter rules for moderation, not a judgment of a person's beliefs or intent. Context, quotes, and reclaimed language need human review.
