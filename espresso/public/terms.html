Terms of Service

Initial version · October 18, 2026

These terms describe the current Espresso Bot MVP. The operator should review them against the final hosting arrangement and intended public service before launch.

About Espresso

Espresso is a Discord moderation bot and settings website operated by Harper. It is an unofficial fan-inspired project, with no affiliation with or endorsement by Sabrina Carpenter or Discord.

Using the service

Use Espresso only in ways permitted by Discord's Terms of Service, community rules, and applicable law. Do not attempt to access servers you do not manage, obtain another person's credentials, bypass authorization checks, or abuse the bot or its infrastructure.

Server administrators' responsibilities

Only add or configure Espresso for servers you are authorized to manage. Choose appropriate filters, exemptions, permissions, and warning thresholds. Explain your server's moderation rules to members and review moderation outcomes. Automated filters can make mistakes and do not replace human judgment.

Accounts and permissions

Website sign-in uses Discord. You remain responsible for securing your Discord account. Settings access requires server ownership, Manage Server, or Administrator permission. Members with Moderate Members may access a limited dashboard for reports and moderation cases; this does not grant configuration access. Removing the bot or revoking access may make server settings unavailable.

Community features

Managers choose which Community hub features to enable and remain responsible for support-team access, public channel selection, event details, saved replies, and role permissions. Members choose whether to request ticket transcripts, subscribe to event reminders, save a birthday, or create a café profile, and may opt out of message highlights. Native Discord events, rooms, messages, reminder delivery, and role cleanup depend on Discord availability and current permissions. Public suggestion updates and community awards are controlled by server staff. Review the Privacy Policy for stored data, retention periods, and deletion controls.

Availability and changes

This is an early project. Features may change, and the service may be unavailable during maintenance, outages, or Discord API interruptions. No specific uptime or moderation accuracy is promised. Keep your own moderation processes and appropriate backups.

Stopping use and data requests

You can sign out of the panel, revoke the application's authorization through Discord's account settings, or remove the bot from a server you manage. See the Privacy Policy for what happens to stored data and how to request deletion. Removing authorization alone does not clear stored moderation records.

Contact

For support or questions about these terms, contact Harper using the contact details at harperurias.com. Include the relevant server ID when asking about a server; never send a bot token or client secret.
